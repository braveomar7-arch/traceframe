OPERATOR HANDBOOK

From evidence to report

8 MINUTE QUICK START

A defensible workflow, built into the tool.

Follow the six-step process below. Traceframe preserves hashes and provenance, while you remain responsible for lawful authority, acquisition method and interpretation.

6operational steps
01

Obtain authority

Confirm scope, legal authority, consent and organizational policy before collecting data.

02

Document the source

Record who collected it, when, where, device state, method and original identifiers.

03

Preserve first

Work from a verified copy when possible. Avoid changing the original and record every transfer.

04

Add evidence

Drop files into Traceframe. Every item is hashed; supported text structures are parsed locally.

05

Review, do not assume

Validate time zones, parser results and entity suggestions against original source evidence.

06

Export and verify

Export the case bundle and manifest. Preserve the application version and document limitations.

Supported now

Hash any file; extract events from JSON, CSV, logs, email text, XML, GPX and HTML; detect emails, phone numbers and coordinates.

Not an acquisition tool

Traceframe does not image disks, bypass security, recover deleted files, or prove authenticity. Use validated acquisition tools first.
